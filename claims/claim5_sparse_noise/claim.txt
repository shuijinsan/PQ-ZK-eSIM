lattice relation. The experiment records which norm check rejects each trial.

  qemu-aarch64-static -L /usr/aarch64-linux-gnu \
      artifact/terminal/euicc/build/arm64/bench_pqzkesim_comprehensive --only sparse
  (200 trials per rho)

Input: none

Expected outputs:
  sparse_noise_attack_results.csv
    columns: rho, detection_rate, false_reject_rate, avg_total_us
  sparse_noise_norm_breakdown.csv
    columns: rho, l2_low_rate, l2_high_rate, linf_rate, l1_low_rate,
             verify_reject_rate

  66% at rho = 90%;
  0% at honest rho = 100%;
  latency about 0.21 ms through rho = 75%, 0.55 ms at rho = 90%, 1.21 ms for
  honest rho = 100%.
  Honest false-rejection rate: no false rejection was observed in the finite
  run. This is a finite-sample observation, not an empirical 2^-128 bound.

  ell_infinity and ell_1 checks.

  - false_reject_rate: 0.0 for honest runs.
  - timing: faster passes; slower up to +100% (2x).
