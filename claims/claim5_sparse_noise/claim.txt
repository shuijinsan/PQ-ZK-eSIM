      artifact/terminal/euicc/build/arm64/bench_pqzkesim_comprehensive --only sparse

Input: none

Expected output:
  sparse_noise_attack_results.csv
    columns: rho, detection_rate, false_reject_rate, avg_total_us

Runtime: ~1 min

Resources: 1 CPU core, <100 MB RAM, <1 MB disk

Tolerance:
  - detection_rate: deterministic at the extremes -- 1.0 for rho <= 0.75 and

    transition band.
  - false_reject_rate: 0.0 for honest runs.
  - timing: faster passes; slower up to +100% (2x).

Notes:

  stated sparsification model; the verifier additionally applies the
  ell_infinity and ell_1 checks. Honest false-rejection is reported
  empirically and is not defined to be exactly zero.
