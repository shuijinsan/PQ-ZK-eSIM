Limitations (disclosed, not hidden):

  - No proprietary commercial eUICC hardware is used.
  - Cortex-A53 results are obtained through ARM64 QEMU emulation.
  - The eUICC latency reported in Table 4 is an analytical projection,
    not an on-card measurement.
  - The TEE/eUICC integration is a research prototype/model,
    not a production GSMA-certified deployment.
  - The Artifact does not provide real biometric-dataset validation
    of the conditional min-entropy assumption.
  - The Artifact is not intended for production deployment.
