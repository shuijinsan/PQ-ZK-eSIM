# Constraints and limitations
- Validated on Ubuntu 22.04 LTS (x86_64); other host platforms are not part of the artifact claim.
- The eUICC path runs under QEMU ARM64 emulation, not on commercial eUICC hardware.
- The analytical eUICC latency projection is not an on-card measurement.
- Concrete lattice security estimates depend on the external lattice-estimator and are not a formal NIST-category assignment.
- The Android frontend is reference software and is not required for the artifact claims.
- The artifact does not empirically validate the biometric conditional-min-entropy assumption.
- The fixed flooding parameter set is evaluated with finite Renyi accounting and is not claimed to instantiate the asymptotic negligible-distance UC condition.
